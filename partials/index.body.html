How it works

Automatic at birth. Locked until adulthood.

  1. Birth registered

    Every eligible child is enrolled automatically from the birth register. No forms, no bank account to open.

  2. BDS $5,000 credited

    The child's share of the Fund is recorded by an independent administrator. Family and friends can add to it.

  3. Invested for growth

    75% in low-cost global index funds, up to 25% in infrastructure, property and private equity, including the Caribbean and Africa.

  4. Claimed as a young adult

    Between 18 and 25 (age to be confirmed), paid into the young person's own bank or credit union account for education, a home or a business.

Where the money is

No single institution controls the money.

The money is split across independent bodies, each checking the others. No bank, minister or manager can move it alone.

Government of BarbadosPays BDS $5,000 per child. Sets the mandate, not the investments.
Child Wealth Fund trustOwns the assets on behalf of every child. Run by independent trustees.
Independent custodianHolds the shares, bonds and fund interests in safekeeping.
Fund administratorKeeps each child's record. Feeds this website.
External auditorChecks the accounts and valuations every year.

This website only shows information. It cannot move money. Claims are paid only into an account in the young person's own name.
