Every Bajan child starts life with BDS $3,000 invested in their future.

A government-funded savings account, held securely by a partner bank, that grows until your child turns 18. Along the way, kids can log in, watch their money grow and learn how saving and investing work.

How it works

Simple, automatic and locked away until it matters.

1

Birth registered

When a qualifying birth is registered, an account is opened automatically in the child's name. No forms, no fees.

2

BDS $3,000 deposited

The Government deposits the starting amount. Families may be able to add more (to be confirmed).

3

Invested for growth

The money is invested in a diversified, low-cost fund that becomes more cautious as the child nears 18.

4

Unlocked at 18

From 18, the young adult can withdraw for approved purposes such as education, training, a first home or starting a business.

Security first

This is children's money and children's data. The live service is designed so that:

The bank holds the money

This site only displays balances. No payments or withdrawals can be made from here.

Strong sign-in

Guardians verify with national ID and two-factor authentication. Children get read-only logins created by their guardian.

Minimal data

No addresses or ID numbers are shown to children. Compliant with the Barbados Data Protection Act 2019.

Questions

Can parents withdraw the money?

No. The money belongs to the child and is locked until 18, apart from exceptional cases set out in law (e.g. terminal illness).

What if my child is born overseas?

To be confirmed in the official rules.

Can the investment lose value?

Investments can go down as well as up in the short term. Over 18 years a diversified fund has historically grown, and the fund becomes more cautious as your child approaches 18.

What happens if nobody claims it at 18?

The money stays invested in the young person's name, and reminders are sent until it is claimed.
