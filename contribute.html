Contribute

Add to a child's future

Give to a named child, or to the Solidarity Pool, which tops up the accounts of children in hardship. Every gift is invested with the Fund and locked until the child is an adult.

Ask the child's parent or guardian. You will never see the child's balance or personal details.

Rules for gifts (proposed)

Tax deduction, named childUp to BDS $2,500 a year per child, max $5,000 a year in total
Tax deduction, Solidarity PoolFully deductible, up to a cap set by the Barbados Revenue Authority
Government matchNone
Can the gift be taken back?No. It belongs to the child
Payment methodsBank transfer, card, regulated remittance. No cash
Large giftsOver BDS $10,000 a year to one child: source-of-funds check

Tax deductions mostly help families who already save. The Solidarity Pool is how the Fund narrows the gap between children instead of widening it.
