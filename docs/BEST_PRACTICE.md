# International best practice: child savings / "baby bond" programmes

| Programme | Design | Lesson for Barbados |
|---|---|---|
| **UK Child Trust Fund** (2002–2011 births) | £250–£500 govt voucher; parents chose a provider, otherwise HMRC allocated one; "stakeholder" funds de-risked from 13 | Over a million accounts went unclaimed at 18 because families lost track. **Build tracing, reminders and a "find my fund" service from day one.** |
| **Singapore Child Development Account** | Opened automatically; first-step govt grant + dollar-for-dollar matching of family savings | Matching creates a saving habit. Consider optional family top-ups with govt match for lower-income families. |
| **Connecticut Baby Bonds** (2023–) | US$3,200 for Medicaid-covered births, held in a state trust; claim 18–30 for home, education, business, retirement | Restricted purposes plus a long claim window protect the money. Requiring financial education before claiming is a good idea. |
| **US "Trump Accounts"** (2025–2028 births) | US$1,000 federal seed; low-cost US index funds (fee cap); family/employer top-ups; locked to 18 | Opt-in leaves children out. **Make enrolment automatic at birth registration.** Fee caps matter. |
| **Hungary Baby Bond** (2006–) | Govt contribution in a locked account to 18 | Universal and simple, but low-yield products eroded real value. |
| **Israel Savings for Every Child** (2017–) | Monthly govt deposit; parents choose risk track; extra bonus if not withdrawn at 18 | A default fund is decisive. The bonus for waiting past 18 encourages longer saving. |

## Design recommendations

1. **Automatic enrolment** at birth registration. No application.
2. **Default lifecycle fund**: diversified, low-cost (fee cap, e.g. ≤0.5%), de-risking from ~age 12.
3. **Locked until 18** with clear approved uses and a claim window (e.g. 18–25) before conversion to a general account.
4. **Tracing and contact**: link to national ID; send annual statements and reminders at 16, 17, 18.
5. **Financial education** built into the portal by age band, and ideally the school curriculum.
6. **Independent trustee/governance** separate from the government of the day; publish annual performance.
7. **Security and anti-fraud** focused on the claim moment (see SECURITY.md).
